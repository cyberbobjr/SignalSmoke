# Sandbox options

Page **Signal Smoke** of the sandbox options.

| Option | Effect | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smoke grenade duration (real seconds) | How long the smoke of a grenade lasts | 90 | 20 – 600 |
| Road flare burn time (in-game minutes) | How long a lit road flare burns | 60 | 10 – 720 |
| Chemlight glow time (in-game hours) | How long an activated chemlight glows | 8 | 1 – 48 |
| Improvised smoke bomb duration (real seconds) | How long the smoke of a homemade bomb lasts | 45 | 10 – 300 |
| Improvised smoke bomb failure chance (%) | Chance that a homemade bomb fizzles | 20 | 0 – 100 |
| Loot multiplier | Multiplies the chance of finding grenades, flares and chemlights | 1.0 | 0 – 10 (0: none) |
| Road flares in police car trunks | Police cars can carry road flares | on | — |

A duration changed during a game applies to the next signals; a smoke, flare or chemlight already burning keeps its duration. Loot options are read again when the game loads.
