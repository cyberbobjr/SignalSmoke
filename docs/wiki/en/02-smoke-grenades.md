# Smoke grenades and improvised smoke bombs

## Smoke grenades

Four colors: **green, red, yellow, purple**. Throw one like the vanilla smoke bomb (range 12 tiles).

- Colored smoke rises on a cross of five tiles for **90 real seconds** (sandbox option), with a hissing sound heard up to 40 tiles away.
- At night, a glow of the same color lights the ground under the smoke.
- No grey smoke, no fire, no damage. Zombies standing in the smoke lose sight of their target, like with vanilla smoke.
- It makes the same noise for zombies as the vanilla smoke bomb.

![Colored smoke at night](../../images/screenshots/shot1.png)

> Capture à faire : green smoke of a grenade in daylight, seen from a distance.

### Where to find them

Army ammunition storage, army bunker storage, army surplus stores and police lockers.

## Improvised smoke bombs

Craft one by hand with the recipe **Make Improvised Smoke Bomb (color)**: green, red, yellow or purple.

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Empty can | 1 |
| Sugar | 2 uses |
| Cold pack **or** fertilizer | 1 use |
| Paint of the chosen color | 1 use |
| Twine, ripped sheets or dirty ripped sheets | 1 |

- Requires **Cooking 2**; gives a little Cooking experience. No recipe to learn.
- Thrown like a grenade (range 10): shorter smoke (**45 real seconds**), and **1 in 5 is a dud** that fizzles without smoke (both are sandbox options).
- Not found as loot.

> Capture à faire : the crafting window with the improvised smoke bomb recipe and its ingredients.
