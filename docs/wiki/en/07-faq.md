# FAQ

**Can I add Signal Smoke to an existing save?**
Yes. Its items appear in containers that have not been looted yet.

**The smoke is missing on some tiles.**
Smoke cannot be placed on water or on a tile with no floor object. When many fires burn nearby, the game's cap of 75 fires and smokes can also remove some smoke tiles; they come back a few seconds later.

**My chemlight does not light up the area in my hands.**
Only a chemlight lying on the ground glows. The time keeps running while you carry it.

**I picked up a lit road flare. Can I light it again?**
No: picking it up puts it out for good.

**A homemade smoke bomb did nothing.**
It was a dud (20 % by default, sandbox option).

**Does the smoke hide me from zombies?**
Zombies standing in the smoke lose sight of their target, as with the vanilla smoke bomb.

**Do other players see my smoke in multiplayer?**
Yes, every player nearby, including those who join later. See [Multiplayer](05-multiplayer.md).

**Where do I report a bug?**
On the Steam Workshop page or the GitHub repository (links on the [Home](Home.md) page).
