# Road flares and chemlights

## Road flares

Right-click a road flare in your inventory: **Light and place on the ground**. Your character crouches, strikes it, and puts it on their tile.

- It burns for **60 minutes of game time** (sandbox option): flickering red light, a little red smoke, a crackling sound. Then it is gone.
- **Pick it up to stop its light.** A picked-up flare gives no light but keeps burning down. Put it back on the ground (drop, place or drag to the floor) near a player: it burns again within a game minute for the time it has left. Once spent, it disappears, on the ground or in your inventory.
- The ground clean-up sandbox option does not remove a burning flare.

Found in gas station emergency supplies, garage tools, fire and police lockers, and police car trunks (sandbox option).

> Capture à faire : a lit road flare on a road at night, with its red light.

## Chemlights

Four colors: **green, red, blue, yellow**. Right-click a new chemlight: **Activate and place on the ground**.

- A soft colored glow (small radius), silent and without smoke, for **8 hours of game time** (sandbox option). It cannot be turned off.
- Pick it up and drop it elsewhere: the time keeps running, and it glows again within a game minute once it is back on the ground near a player (drop, place or drag to the floor all work).
- When it runs out it becomes a **Used Chemlight**, on the ground or in your bags.
- A chemlight only lights the area when it lies on the ground, not in your hands.

![Chemlights glowing on the ground at night](../../images/screenshots/shot2.png)

Found in army storage and surplus stores, police and fire lockers, camping stores and survival gear.
