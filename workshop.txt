title=Signal Smoke - Colored Smoke Grenades, Flares & Chemlights
description=[h1]Signal Smoke — Colored Smoke Grenades, Road Flares & Chemlights[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer[/b]
description=
description=Need a helicopter to spot your roof, a friend to find your camp, or a way to mark the road home? Pop colored smoke, light a road flare or drop a chemlight: seen by everyone.
description=
description=[h2]At a glance[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Four smoke grenades[/b]: green, red, yellow and purple, with their own 3D models and icons.
description=[*][b]Road flares[/b] that burn red on the ground for about an hour of game time.
description=[*][b]Chemlights[/b] in four colors: a quiet glow that lasts for hours, to mark a path or a basement.
description=[*][b]Improvised smoke bombs[/b]: craft your own colored smoke.
description=[*][b]Sandbox options[/b]: durations, dud chance, loot.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer[/b]: the server decides, every player sees the same smoke and flares.
description=[*][b]A small library for other mods[/b]: colored smoke and flares placed by script.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Smoke grenades[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Throw it like the vanilla smoke bomb: colored smoke rises over a small cross of tiles for about a minute and a half, visible from afar. At night, it glows in its color.
description=[*]Zombies standing in the smoke lose sight of their target.
description=[*]No grey smoke, no fire, no damage: it is a signal, not a weapon.
description=[*]Found in army storage, army surplus stores and police lockers.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Road flares[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Right-click the flare, [b]Light and place on the ground[/b].
description=[*]It burns with a flickering red light, a little red smoke and a crackling sound for about an hour of game time, then it is gone.
description=[*]Pick it up to put it out.
description=[*]Found in gas station emergency supplies, car supply tools, fire department and police lockers.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Chemlights[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Green, red, blue or yellow. Right-click, [b]Activate and place on the ground[/b].
description=[*]A small colored glow (about 3 tiles), with no smoke and no sound, for 8 hours of game time.
description=[*]Once activated it can't be switched off: pick it up and drop it elsewhere, it keeps glowing until it runs out.
description=[*]Found in army storage and surplus stores, police and fire lockers, camping and survival gear.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Improvised smoke bombs[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Recipe [b]Make Improvised Smoke Bomb[/b] (Cooking 2): an empty can, sugar, a cold pack or fertilizer, paint in the color you want (green, red, yellow or purple) and a fuse (twine or a fabric strip).
description=[*]Thrown like a smoke grenade, but less reliable: shorter smoke, and 1 in 5 is a dud.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=
description=Smoke, flare and chemlight durations, improvised smoke duration and dud chance, loot multiplier, flares in police car trunks.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=
description=The server keeps the list of active smokes and flares and sends it to every client: players who arrive later see them too, and they come back after loading a save.
description=
description=[h2]For modders[/h2]
description=
description=Add [b]require=\batman_SignalSmoke[/b] to your mod.info, then, on the server or in singleplayer:
description=[code]
description=local SignalSmoke = require "SignalSmoke/SignalSmoke"
description=local id = SignalSmoke.start{ x = 100, y = 200, z = 0, color = "green", minutes = 30 }
description=SignalSmoke.stop(id)
description=[/code]
description=Named colors: green, red, yellow, purple, orange, blue, white, or any { r, g, b } table. Use kind = "flare" for a road flare, kind = "chemlight" for a chemlight.
description=[b]Events[/b]: SignalSmoke.onSignal(function(event) ... end) is called on the server when a grenade, flare, chemlight or script signal starts, stops or expires (type, id, entry, source, username). Full API on GitHub.
